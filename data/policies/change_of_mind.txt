Change Of Mind Policy

Customers may return items for change of mind within 14 days of delivery.
Eligible only if: product is unused, in original packaging, with all accessories.
Refund amount equals product price only (shipping not refunded).
Return shipping cost is deducted from refund ($9.99 flat rate).
Customer must initiate return via the returns portal.
Refund issued after warehouse receives and inspects the return.
