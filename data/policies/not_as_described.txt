Not As Described Policy

Customers are eligible for a refund if the product materially differs from the description.
This includes: wrong color, wrong size, missing features advertised, different material than described.
The discrepancy must be reported within 30 days of delivery.
Evidence required: photos showing the difference vs. product listing.
Refund amount equals the full order amount.
Return shipping is covered by the seller if the error is on our side.
