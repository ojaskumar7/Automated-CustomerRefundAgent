Late Delivery Policy

Customers may request a refund if delivery exceeds the estimated delivery window by more than 7 days.
Eligible for: partial refund (15% of order amount) for delays 7-14 days, full refund for delays >14 days.
The late delivery must not be due to customer-provided incorrect address or refused delivery.
Refund requested within 30 days of the original estimated delivery date.
No evidence required beyond tracking confirmation of late delivery.
Shipping costs are not refunded for late delivery (only product amount).
