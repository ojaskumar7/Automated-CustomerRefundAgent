Billing Error Policy

Customers are eligible for a refund if they were charged incorrectly.
This includes: duplicate charges, wrong amount charged, charged for cancelled order, currency conversion errors.
Must be reported within 60 days of the charge.
Evidence required: bank statement showing the erroneous charge.
Refund amount equals the overcharged amount only.
Full order refund only if the entire charge was erroneous (e.g., cancelled order still charged).
