Defective Product Policy

Customers are eligible for a full refund if the product arrives with a manufacturing defect.
This includes: broken screens, missing components, non-functional hardware, damaged packaging that caused product damage.
The defect must be reported within 30 days of delivery.
Evidence required: clear photos of the defect and packaging.
Refund amount equals the full order amount including tax.
Shipping costs are refunded if the defect is verified as manufacturer responsibility.
